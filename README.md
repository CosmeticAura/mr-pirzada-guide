# When Mr. Pirzada Came to Dine: techniques and conflicts

A one-page study guide for Jhumpa Lahiri's short story "When Mr. Pirzada Came to Dine".

It covers:
- External and internal conflicts, with the moments from the story
- Nine literary techniques: symbolism, irony, foreshadowing, imagery, motif, contrast, point of view, characterization and setting
- 12 flashcards that loop until every card is right

Open the page: https://cosmeticaura.github.io/mr-pirzada-guide/

Tip: study in 25 minute blocks with a 5 minute break.
